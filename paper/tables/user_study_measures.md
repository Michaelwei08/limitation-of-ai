# Planned User-Study Measures

Status: design artifact for a future non-expert study. This table reports no
participant results, effect sizes, or statistical tests.

Standardized instrument use is planned, not finalized. If workload is measured
with NASA-TLX, raw NASA-TLX, or a similar validated scale, the exact instrument,
items, and scoring procedure must be finalized before recruitment.

| Measure | Type | Collection point | HCI/privacy RQ mapping |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prompt comprehension accuracy | Quantitative closed-form accuracy across allowed data, blocked data, redaction, confirmation, external disclosure, untrusted instructions, and audit-summary items. | Main comprehension items and optional audit-summary interpretation. | RQ1; supports RQ4 when audit and control cues are interpreted correctly. |
| Correct allow/deny decision | Quantitative answer-key match for allow, deny, narrow, require redaction, or require confirmation. | Main permission decision after each scenario prompt. | RQ2 and RQ3. |
| False allow | Quantitative privacy-failure rate when a participant permits unsafe, unrelated, unredacted, over-broad, prompt-injected, or unconfirmed high-risk action. | Main permission decision after each scenario prompt. | RQ2 and RQ3. |
| False denial | Quantitative utility-failure rate when a participant blocks necessary low-risk or properly gated access. | Main permission decision after each scenario prompt. | RQ2, RQ3, and RQ5. |
| Time to decision | Quantitative elapsed time from prompt display to final decision. | Prompt display timestamp and submitted permission decision. | RQ3 and RQ5. |
| Perceived control | Planned quantitative self-report, with exact items not finalized. | Post-scenario ratings and end-of-study survey. | RQ4. |
| Perceived trust | Planned quantitative self-report, with exact items not finalized. | Post-scenario ratings and end-of-study survey. | RQ4. |
| Consent fatigue | Planned quantitative self-report about repeated approvals, interruption, annoyance, and reading effort; may be paired with prompt and confirmation counts. | Post-scenario ratings and end-of-study survey. | RQ5. |
| Workload | Planned NASA-TLX, raw NASA-TLX, or similar workload measure if appropriate; exact instrument remains planned. | Post-scenario ratings or end-of-study survey. | RQ5. |
| Interview responses | Qualitative optional interview or free-response data about how participants interpreted the permission boundary. | Optional interview or end-of-study free response. | RQ1, RQ4, and RQ5. |
| Confusion points | Qualitative coding of unclear wording, missing context, unclear recipients, redaction state, confirmation state, or untrusted-content handling. | Free response, post-scenario notes, or interview. | RQ1 and RQ5. |
| Reasons for allowing or denying | Qualitative coding of decision rationale, including privacy concern, task utility, trust, redaction awareness, confirmation awareness, or misunderstanding. | One-sentence rationale after selected permission decisions and optional interview. | RQ2, RQ3, and RQ4. |
| Prompt wording and detail-level preferences | Qualitative preference data about concise, detailed, risk-highlighted, expandable, or audit-preview prompt formats. | End-of-study survey and optional interview. | RQ1, RQ4, and RQ5. |
