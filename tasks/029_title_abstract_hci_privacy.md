# Task 029: Update Title and Abstract

Status: planned.

## Goal

Update the title and abstract to signal an HCI/privacy paper.

## Create or update

- `paper/papf_final.md`
- `docs/paper_outline.md`

## Requirements

- Choose or test a title direction such as:
  - "Task-Scoped Consent for Personal AI Agents",
  - "Designing User-Comprehensible Permissions for Consumer AI Agents",
  - "Human-Centered Permission Boundaries for Personal AI Agents",
  - "External Enforcement and Consent UX for Privacy-Preserving AI Agents".
- Abstract must clearly state:
  - problem: personal AI agents have broad tool and data access,
  - approach: PAPF enforces task-scoped authority outside the model,
  - evaluation: prototype trace evaluation plus user study of permission comprehension and consent burden,
  - contribution: architecture, prompt design, and empirical or planned privacy-usability findings.
- If the user study has not been run, abstract must say "we design" or "we plan to evaluate", not "we find".

## Verification

- Title and abstract match the HCI/privacy framing.
- Abstract does not overstate prototype maturity or user-comprehension evidence.
- Abstract mentions consent burden or privacy usability explicitly.
